Add a category

Anything applied to the body is a cosmetic under the UK Cosmetics Regulation. It needs a UK Responsible Person, a safety assessment and product notification before it can be sold. That works only by buying from a UK supplier who already holds that role — own-brand puts the entire burden on Packet. The category will be created with that flag set, and the sourcing tool will refuse to shortlist anything in it from a supplier who does not hold RP. This is a prompt for a solicitor, not advice.

Shop by shop

Works with every supplier from day one, whether or not they offer a feed: export their price list, paste it in, done. Suppliers with a real feed get wired up properly later — this is the path that never depends on them having one.

sku and title and cost_price are required; the rest can be left out. Prices go in as pounds — 1.10, not 110. cost_price is the trade price excluding VAT. gtin is the barcode, and it is worth more than every other column put together: with it, a product matches a competitor exactly, and without it the tool is comparing titles and guessing.

These start empty deliberately, and the tool will not price anything until they are filled in. A sensible-looking default would get quoted back later as though somebody had checked it. Take them from the real Shopify plan, the real payment contract and the returns you have actually seen — and if you do not know one yet, leave it blank rather than estimating. Blank is reported honestly. A guess is not.

Advertising

The line that decides whether any of this is a business. A product can look healthy and be underwater the moment it costs money to find a buyer. Left blank, every figure on the shortlist is marked as being before advertising — which is a warning, not a pass.

Where we price

How early a season starts appearing on the Seasonal tab. This one has a sensible default of 26 because it only decides what is on the screen, not what anything is worth — unlike everything above it.

Categories and returns

Return rates stay blank until you have measured one or a supplier has given you theirs in writing. Blank means returns are left out of the margin entirely and the row is marked best case — which is the honest way to show a number nobody has yet.
