Sourcing

What the competitors are selling, what the suppliers can supply, and where the two overlap at a price worth having. Recommendations only — nothing goes near the shop until you say so.

Packet is the seller of everything it lists, whoever ships the box. What is recorded here decides what the tool will and will not put in front of you — in particular the Responsible Person question, which blocks cosmetics outright when the answer is no.

Add a supplier

Anything applied to the body — shampoo, skincare, nails, fragrance — needs a UK Responsible Person, a safety assessment and a product notification. Leave this unticked and the tool will refuse to shortlist their cosmetics, which is the point of it. Tools and brushes are unaffected. This is a note for your solicitor to confirm, not legal advice.

Shopify shops publish a product feed that anyone can read, which is why they are straightforward to watch. Anything else has to be followed by hand. Check a shop’s terms before adding it — reading a public price is ordinary research, but this should stay a weekly look, never a crawl.

How each competitor actually operates — the shape of their range, where they sit on price, how fast they add things, and what they promise on delivery and returns. Every number here is counted from pages we have read. Only the written summaries are AI, and only about those numbers.

What this cannot see: their sales, their profit, their advertising spend, their visitor numbers, or who supplies them. All private. So it can tell you what a shop does — never how well it is doing. A tidy site with 400 products might be on its last legs.

Shop by shop

Works with every supplier from day one, whether or not they offer a feed: export their price list, paste it in, done. Suppliers with a real feed get wired up properly later — this is the path that never depends on them having one.

sku and title and cost_price are required; the rest can be left out. Prices go in as pounds — 1.10, not 110. cost_price is the trade price excluding VAT. gtin is the barcode, and it is worth more than every other column put together: with it, a product matches a competitor exactly, and without it the tool is comparing titles and guessing.

These start empty deliberately, and the tool will not price anything until they are filled in. A sensible-looking default would get quoted back later as though somebody had checked it. Take them from the real Shopify plan, the real payment contract and the returns you have actually seen — and if you do not know one yet, leave it blank rather than estimating. Blank is reported honestly. A guess is not.

The line that decides whether any of this is a business. A product can look healthy and be underwater the moment it costs money to find a buyer. Left blank, every figure on the shortlist is marked as being before advertising — which is a warning, not a pass.
