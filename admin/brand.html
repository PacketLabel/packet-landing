How to choose what comes across

In your own Shopify admin, add the tag packet-outlet to any product you want on Packet Outlet. Every size and colour of it comes across, with your photos and description. Take the tag off, archive it or sell out, and Packet follows within ten minutes.

Optional tags, if the defaults aren’t right for a product:

How it works

1. You add stock. Returns, end-of-season lines, packaging-damaged items — anything that’s hard to sell at full price on your own site. Tell us its condition honestly and the lowest price you’ll take.


2. We check it and put it on sale. We set the Outlet price, never below your lowest. You’ll see it change to On sale. If we can’t list something we’ll say why on the item.


3. When it sells, you post it. The order appears under Orders with the delivery address. Post it straight to the customer and put the tracking number in.


4. We pay you. The Money page shows what each sale earns you and what’s been paid.


You never send us stock. If you sell something yourself, just lower the number or take it back — press Take back on the item. There is no minimum.
